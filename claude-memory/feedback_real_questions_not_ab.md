Since 2026-11-18: posts end with a real question the reader answers in a sentence (the user's own example: "¿Cómo conociste a Margaret Atwood: por el libro o por la serie?"), never "comenta A o B" or one-letter answers. Add a reason to save or send the post.

**Why:** the user dislikes A/B prompts. They read as engagement bait, don't fit the "yo", passionate voice, and comments from existing viewers rarely become followers; sends and saves reach new people.

**How to apply:** replaces the old "one easy answer" rule in MARKETING.md (rewritten there and in the social-content skill). Related: [[feedback-social-voice-i-underdog]], [[project-social-acquisition-reading-tool]], [[feedback-book-covers-in-posts]].
