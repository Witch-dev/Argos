Since 2026-11-16: Instagram posts about a writer or their books (anniversaries, prize news, "empieza por aquí" guides) use the books' real covers from Open Library, a Spanish edition when one has a cover, uploaded to the design canvas as assets, with alt text naming the edition. Posts about Winged Words itself keep screenshots, code drawings and public-domain art.

**Why:** the user said "we are talking about the writer and books and not our app". They were told once that modern cover art belongs to the publisher and accepted the risk; don't raise it again for every post.

**How to apply:** search `openlibrary.org/search.json` for editions with `cover_i`, compare candidates on a contact sheet, skip tiny (<300px wide) or children's-edition covers. Rules written in MARKETING.md and the social-content skill. Related: [[feedback-social-voice-i-underdog]], [[project-spanish-speaking-users]].
