For social media posts (captions, stories, replies, bio) write as one independent person, "yo" not "nosotros", passionate about books, and lean into being the underdog.

**Why:** 2026-11-07 the user asked about company vs personal voice; research (Packard 2018 on "I" vs "we", Paharia 2011 underdog effect) backed the personal voice. The user decided: "I" and passionate is the guideline for all posts I'm asked to create.

**How to apply:** lead with passion for reading and building, mention being small in one honest line, no hardship story or apologising, never hide that it's one person. "Nosotros" only for readers together. App UI and legal pages stay neutral. Rules live in Argos/MARKETING.md "Voice on social media" and the social-content skill. See [[spanish-speaking-users]].
