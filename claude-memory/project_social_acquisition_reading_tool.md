2026-11-07: the user reminded me the Instagram account has few followers. Posts must bring new people in (reach), not maintain existing followers, so they need to be strong enough for Instagram to push. They also want to push the idea of Winged Words as a tool that promotes reading.

**Why:** feature-only posts (half stars, goal cards) serve people who already follow; a tiny account grows through posts people save, send and tag.

**How to apply:** lead with a relatable reading problem or a tag/share prompt ("etiqueta a quien…"), put the product in the last slide or last line as "hice Winged Words para leer más", and favour Reels and carousels for reach. Feature posts come second. Keep the usual rules in MARKETING.md (Spanish, "yo", only true claims, no invented numbers). See [[social-voice-i-underdog]].
