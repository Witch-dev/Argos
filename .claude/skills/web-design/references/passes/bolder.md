# Pass: bolder

Raise **one named part** to the conviction the rest of the site already has. Not more effects: reaching for more effects is the opposite of bold, so reject that reflex first.

## Scope is sovereign
- Touch only the named target. Neighbours, the page and the system stay as they are.
- No new colours, fonts, radii, shadows or tokens. If DESIGN.md genuinely can't express the direction, stop and ask, naming the exact addition and the job it would do.

## Why it reads flat
A flat section usually opts out of moves its neighbours already make. Compare it with the strongest parts of the site: display type at full strength, the shelf, raised cards, the green accent where it carries meaning, density and pacing. The reliable bolder pass brings the target up to that level **in our own vocabulary**.

## How
- **Amplify what the system owns:** the shelf, Fraunces/Literata display sizes at full strength, the stat tile's big number, a book cover at a real size, a raised card. The bolder version looks *more* like Winged Words, not less.
- **Commit, then clarify:** make one decisive move completely, then quiet everything around it so the move reads. If every element got louder, the section got flatter.
- **Give it its own rhythm:** a peak in the scroll, a change in density or pace, not more of the same.
- **Keep content true:** no invented numbers, quotes or claims; ask for real ones.

## The skeleton test
Strip the copy from the plan. Does the bare structure still say what the section is and why it matters, through hierarchy and the system's devices? If it only works once the words return, the boldness is in the text size, not the design.

## Before you finish
- Everything outside the target is unchanged; no new primitives appeared.
- Controls in the section still work the same way.
- It is unmistakably Winged Words, only surer of itself. Then `polish`.
