# Pass: delight

Make a moment memorable through a useful interaction, a humane response or a carefully considered detail: personality that serves the task. On Winged Words' Operate and Read screens delight lives at a few moments (first use, finishing a book, reaching the yearly goal, recovering from an error); reliability carries everything else.

## Process
1. **Find the opportunity:** read PRODUCT.md (voice, feel) and DESIGN.md, how often the moment happens and how the reader feels then. Look for effort worth acknowledging, waiting that can inform, empty states that can orient, errors that need care.
2. **Write one delight thesis:** one sentence on what the reader should feel and why it fits Winged Words.
3. **Choose the smallest thing that delivers it:** a distinctive response, our own words, a material-like transition (the shelf), a discovery reward.

## By moment
- **Success:** match the response to the effort. Finishing a book or the yearly goal may expand; a routine save should just feel certain.
- **Waiting:** truthful progress or real activity; never fake work or delay completion for effect.
- **Empty and first use:** make the next action clear before adding personality.
- **Errors:** lead with the problem and the fix; warmth is welcome, jokes about lost data, privacy or blocked work are not.
- **Repeated use:** still right the hundredth time; any variation stays predictable.
- **Discovery:** reward curiosity without hiding anything a reader needs.

## Never
Delay, block or hide the task; override platform conventions or accessibility; invent facts; play sound; become unskippable or tiring on repeat; celebrate ordinary clicks.

## Check
Specific to Winged Words, improves understanding or confidence, works without the flourish, fine on repeat, works muted, by keyboard, by touch and in all five languages. Then `polish`.
