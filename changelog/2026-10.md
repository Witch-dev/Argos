### 2026-10-03 — Find readers, Phase 3: book-page readers, buddy reads and club invites, genre/audience/mood filters (spec complete)

Implemented Phase 3, the last phase of `specs/find-readers-discovery.md`.

**Backend:**
- **Genres:** `GenreCatalog` (22 genres in fiction/nonfiction groups, 4 audiences) classifies books from Open Library subjects into `Book.Genres`/`Audience`, on cache and refresh plus a startup backfill. Migration `AddBookGenresAndClubInvites` was applied to the dev database. A spot-check of real books showed the first rules gave popular books up to 12 genres, so they were tuned: at most 3 genres ranked by matching subjects, adaptations ignored, and young-reader audiences only when those tags are a real share of the subjects (details in the spec §3.7).
- **Filters:** `GET /api/discover/browse` (genre or mood in a reader's top 3, main audience, mostly fiction or nonfiction) and `GET /api/discover/filters`. Reader cards gained `topGenres`.
- **Book readers:** `GET /api/books/{olid}/readers` returns "loved it" (newest visible rating 4.5+) and "reading now", followed first, blocked and hidden readers left out.
- **Club invites:** a new `Invited` membership status, never treated as a member. Invite, my invites, accept and decline endpoints; joining by link or from the directory accepts an invite.
- **Buddy reads:** `CreateClubRequest` gained book, finish date and invitees. A buddy-read club is created already reading the book, with one "Finish the book" checkpoint, in one transaction (`ClubInviteService`).

**Frontend:**
- A filter bar on Find readers, kept in the URL.
- Genre tags on reader cards.
- A "Readers" section on book pages with "Start a buddy read".
- The create-club modal prefilled for buddy reads (book chip, Finish by, invitee chips).
- A "You're invited" strip on the Clubs page, plus a dot on the Clubs menu item.
- "Invite readers" (search, pending invites, cancel) on the club page for admins and moderators.

Backend 295 → 314 tests, frontend 352 → 360, all passing; build and eslint clean. Driven live in Chromium end to end, including accepting an invite as the invited account.

### 2026-10-03 — Find readers, Phase 2: taste match, readers like you, compare page

Implemented Phase 2 of `specs/find-readers-discovery.md`.

**Backend:**
- `TasteMatchService` scores how closely two readers' ratings agree: `100 × (1 − average gap ÷ 4.5)` over books both rated, shown only from 3 books in common.
- The other reader's ratings only count if you could see them on their review (Public, or Followers-only when you follow them). A reread uses the newest rating.
- Reader cards now carry `match`.
- New endpoints: `GET /api/discover/like-you` (best matches, with the same exclusions as the other sections), `GET /api/users/{username}/match` for the profile badge, and `GET /api/users/{username}/compare`. Compare returns books you've both rated, up to 5 disagreements of 1.5★ or more, and books they rated 4.5+ that are on your want-to-read shelf.
- No schema change; computed on request.

**Frontend:**
- "Readers like you" is the first Find readers section, and it nudges you to rate more books when empty.
- A `MatchBadge` ("82% match · 14 in common") shows on cards and profile headers.
- A new compare page at `/u/:username/compare`: the match and average gap in words, disagreements, "they loved it, it's on your to-read", and shared books sortable by title or biggest difference.

Backend 288 → 295 tests (3 formula unit tests, 4 integration), frontend 347 → 352, all passing; build and eslint clean. Driven live in Chromium with seeded `verify_find_*` accounts, including dark theme and 390px width.

### 2026-10-03 — Blocking hides the blocker's profile from the blocked reader

When A blocks B, B now gets "not found" for A's profile, favourites, shelves, lists, reviews-by-author and followers/following lists, the way Letterboxd and Goodreads handle it (`BlockService.IsHiddenFromAsync`, applied in the Users, Logs, BookLists, Reviews and Follows controllers). It's one-way, so A can still open B's profile to unblock, and signed-out visitors still see everything. The user chose this after the security review raised it. ### 2026-10-01 — Fix: blurry, cropped book covers on post cards

Writing/post cards showed a zoomed-in, pixelated slice of the cover. Two causes: the backend stores Open Library's medium cover (`-M.jpg`, ~180px), which got stretched across the full card, and the square slot used `object-fit: cover`, which cut a 2:3 cover down to its top third. `BookCover size="square"` now shows the whole cover centered over a blurred, darkened copy of itself, and asks for the large image (`-L.jpg`, via the new `lib/coverUrl.ts`). Other cover sizes still use the medium image. Frontend 282 → 285 tests, all passing; tsc and eslint clean. Not yet checked in a browser. Second follow-up: writing cards got space above them as well as below (`padding-block: var(--space-6)`), so the divider no longer sits right on top of the next post's image. We chose spacing over boxed or tinted cards to stay consistent with the feed's other entries, which are separated by lines.
