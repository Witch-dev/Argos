# Social calendar: late November to early December 2026

Literary dates for Instagram posts (`@wingedwords.app`), Spanish only, Latin American authors first. Made 2026-11-13, after the Stevenson carousel. Pace: 2–3 posts a week, so each week has **main** posts and some **optional** ones. Drafts are made with the `social-content` skill on the design canvas; nothing is posted automatically.

**Format that worked for Stevenson:** "Si nunca lo leíste, empieza por aquí", three books from shortest to longest, a closing fact, and "¿Cuál ya leíste? Comenta 1, 2 o 3". Reuse it for big names; use a single post or a question for smaller dates so the feed doesn't repeat.

**Images:** real book covers from Open Library, a Spanish edition when there is one (rule since 2026-11-16, MARKETING.md). Quotes only from public-domain authors (died before ~1956): Silva, Tolstoy, Collodi, Bello, Twain, Wilde, Alcott. Saramago, Piglia, Revueltas, Atwood, Lispector and C.S. Lewis are still under copyright: describe their books, don't quote them.

## Facts to check when making each post

Checked by web search on 2026-11-13:
- Saramago: born 16 November 1922 in Azinhaga, Portugal; his official papers say the 18th ([Nobel Prize biography](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/literature/1998/saramago/biographical/)).
- Revueltas: born 20 November 1914 in Durango ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Jose-Revueltas)).
- Piglia: born 24 November 1941 in Adrogué; some sources say 1940, and he changed the year himself on some book flaps ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Ricardo-Piglia)).
- Collodi: born 24 November 1826 in Florence ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/C-Collodi)).
- José Asunción Silva: born 27 November 1865 in Bogotá ([Banco de la República](https://www.banrep.gov.co/es/billetes/billete-5000-pesos-edicion-conmemorativa-1995)).
- Andrés Bello: born 29 November 1781 in Caracas (baptism record; he himself sometimes said 1780) ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/biography/Andres-Bello)).
- FIL Guadalajara 2026: 28 November – 6 December, Expo Guadalajara, 40th edition, Italy as guest ([Telediario](https://www.telediario.mx/comunidad/cuando-es-la-fil-guadalajara-2026)). Check fil.com.mx closer to the date.
- Lispector: born 10 December 1920 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clarice_Lispector)).

Well known but not re-checked: Lindgren 14 Nov 1907, Atwood 18 Nov 1939, Tolstoy died 20 Nov 1910, C.S. Lewis and Alcott 29 Nov (1898, 1832), Twain 30 Nov 1835, Wilde died 30 Nov 1900. Also check book details (the cricket in Pinocchio, which books are short) before writing the slides.
